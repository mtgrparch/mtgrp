Legal basis: our legitimate interest in publishing a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

2. Weather in the page footer

The current temperature shown next to Beirut, Madrid and Milan is fetched directly by your browser from Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Open-Meteo therefore receives your IP address when the page loads. It doesn't use cookies or require an account, and we send it nothing else about you.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in showing this information (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you block requests to api.open-meteo.com, the site works normally without the temperatures.
