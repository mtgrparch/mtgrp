Dancing House

Adaptive Reuse & Coliving
LocationBeit Mery, LB
TypeResidential / Coliving
Team

A collaborative adaptive project for a traditional house in Beit Mery. The existing structure is adapted for a coliving situation with communal activities, and the garden is populated with a series of follies that extend the collective life of the house into the landscape.
