Competition entry for the Holcim Visitor Center. Positioned as a precise interface between industrial production and the public, the proposal engages with the material logic of cement. The building acts as a transparent, functional framework rather than a conventional shell, performing its own material origin while physically manifesting the circular economy and flexible, future-proof architectural design.
