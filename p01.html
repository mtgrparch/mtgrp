A Home in the Sun

Coliving for Seniors

An elderly coliving home for seniors in Lysá nad Labem, Czech Republic. The project proposes a shared domestic environment that rethinks how aging populations inhabit collective space — prioritising thermal comfort, communal activity, and open-ended use over institutional typology. Warmth, both climatic and social, is the organising principle.
